Status: Story O accepted after real Qwen3 4B regression review.

## Frozen Failures

The Story L 4B baseline gave 3/4 to an answer whose transformation `2x=6` was correct but whose final result was explicitly `x=4`; Gold was 1/4. The Story I smoke also showed that Chinese questions could receive English feedback.

Baseline SHA256 values were `0ca7ea64b481cc5aebd3840917053b98dabc45dd13d7f000b4f64e7960a7b4f8` for the local structured prompt and `d8d264145a018c44c2c7459073714284b2245874f5a779fd93193dcd2dfcbe53` for the calculation prompt.

## v2 Changes

- Prompt v2 states that evidence presence is not semantic entailment and contradictions make a point missing.
- Calculation v2 independently checks transformation, result, substitution, sign, unit, and tolerance.
- Rubric points may declare `match_policy=semantic` or `strict_alias`.
- Strict-alias points that lack an approved alias in the answer are downgraded by code, with their evidence removed.
- Student feedback is generated by code in the question language and cannot contradict mandatory review.

## Acceptance

The real 4B must score the contradictory answer 1/4, preserve 4/4 on the full calculation control, preserve 2/2 evidence on the Chinese control, emit Chinese feedback, and pass schema/evidence checks on all frozen cases.
