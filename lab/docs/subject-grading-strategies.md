# Subject Grading Strategies

Subject strategies are implemented in `src/subjects.js`.

## Math

Focus on steps, numeric tolerance, units, and result. Do not award full credit for final answer only when steps are required.

## Physics

Require concept evidence, relation to quantities, and units where relevant. Review vague force-only answers.

## Chemistry

Require reaction, formula, phenomenon, or concept evidence. Review unsafe or unsupported experimental claims.

## Biology

Require process, structure, function, or key-term evidence. Review answers that use memorized terms without relation.

## English

Use dimensional rubrics for content, organization, and language. Essays require human review. Do not reward fluency without rubric evidence.

## Chinese

Require textual detail and interpretation evidence. Do not reward length alone.

## History

Require event, cause, consequence, or evidence. Review claims without historical support.

## Politics

Require concept and argument evidence. Review slogans without reasoning.

## Geography

Require spatial, process, and cause-effect evidence. Review missing location or mechanism.
